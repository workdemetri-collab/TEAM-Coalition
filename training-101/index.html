Team Training 101

Alcohol Management Training

TEAM Coalition's training program in effective alcohol management is the profit center for the organization, accounting for $600,000 to $700,000 in profit annually. Revenue from training comes in three forms: trainer certification, employee certification and rush/special processing.

Trainer Certification

Trainer Instructor Development Program Workshops (IDPs) certify approximately 350 facility, guest services and concessions managers annually. These two-day workshops bring new trainers and recertifying trainers together to share best practices on alcohol management experiences and how best to train sports and entertainment facility employees. Approximately 100 trainers recertify online each year. Trainers qualify for the online recertification exam by training at least 80 employees or 4 sessions during their 24-month certification period. Trainers cannot recertify online if their last recertification was online. Instead they must attend another IDP.

With Covid significantly affecting the sports and entertainment industry, only 296 facility managers became certified TEAM trainers in fiscal year 2020 (October 1, 2019 to September 30, 2020). In fiscal year 2019 (October 1, 2018 to September 30, 2019) 375 facility managers became certified TEAM trainers.

Member Benefits:

TEAM Coalition offers:
  • Unique collaboration across industry segments and public entities to share best practices and create systemic change
  • Full-facility, customized alcohol management training which turns risk reduction into an improved guest experience
  • Consumer programs that empower responsible fan behavior through branded opportunities and incredible rewards
All TEAM Coalition members contribute $15,000 annually in membership dues.

TEAM Coalition member organizations receive significant savings off the price of trainer certification.

  • Member customers like Aramark, Legends, Spectra, Delaware North and Live Nation save $250 per new trainer certified at an IDP as compared to non members.
  • Member affiliates like NFL teams, NASCAR tracks and NCAA universities save $200 per new trainer certified at an IDP.

Employee Certification

Certifying facility employees in TEAM's alcohol management training program is the single most significant source of revenue for the organization. Regardless of job function, TEAM training is designed to provide all employees the basic knowledge in the signs of alcohol impairment, absorption rate factors and legal liability in addition to customized knowledge of the policies, procedures and protocol of the specific alcohol management plan at the facility where they work. In TEAM Coalition's history, the most employees certified in a single fiscal year was 64,094. That was FY 2016. In FY 2019, 50,817 employees of sports and entertainment facilities became TEAM certified. And because of the impact of Covid on the sports and entertainment industry only 13,540 employees were certified in FY 2020.

In order to certify their employees, trainers must purchase Participant Manuals from TEAM Coalition. They are sold in bundles of 10. In FY 2019, 56,700 TEAM Training Participant Manuals were sold. In FY 2020, only 20,929 TEAM Training Participant Manuals were sold. They are available in English and Spanish. Included with the Participant Manuals are the exam forms. Trainers mail the exam forms to TEAM for processing and grading.

There is no cost for standard processing. Rush processing comes with a fee and represents approximately $7,500 to $10,000 in annual revenue for the organization.

Employees who serve alcohol must complete TEAM Level 2. Employees who do not touch alcohol may complete TEAM Level 1. But all are encouraged to complete TEAM Level 2. There is no price difference between the two products. The Level 2 program requires an additional hour of lecture as compared to the 2-hour Level 1 course, and the certification test has 30 questions instead of 20. So trainers will see greater costs for employee time with TEAM Level 2. For both levels, employee certification is good for 3 years, except in Maryland where regulations require employee certification be valid for 4 years.

  • Member customers like Aramark, Legends, Spectra, Delaware North and Live Nation save $3 per Participant Manual as compared to non members.
  • Member affiliates like NFL teams, NASCAR tracks and NCAA universities save $2 per Participant Manual.
