TEAM Coalition and Major League Soccer support the responsible fan programs at MLS stadiums every season with a special incentive called the Responsibility Has Its Rewards (RHIR) sweepstakes. Supporters who register to be a responsible fan pledge to drink alcohol responsibly, find a safe ride home, and always buckle up.. At the end of the season, one Responsible Fan for the Season from each Club wasselected. The Responsible Fans for the Season from the Clubs that compete in MLS CupTM each received the Grand Prize of the Sweepstakes. The Grand Prize of the Sweepstakes was a trip for two (2) to MLS Cup including two (2) tickets to the game, round trip airfare and hotel accommodations (if necessary). A First Prize for the RHIR sweepstakes is also awarded. The remaining Responsible Fans for the Season whose Clubs did not compete in MLS Cup are qualified to win the first prize. The first prize was a trip to MLS All-Star Game during the next MLS season. That winners have been chosen randomly from the remaining Responsible Fans for the Season. The All-Star Game® trip includes two (2) tickets to the game, round trip airfare, and hotel accommodations (if necessary). Click here to see the current RHIR sweepstakes with MLS.
