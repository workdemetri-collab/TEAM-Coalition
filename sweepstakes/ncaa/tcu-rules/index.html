OFFICIAL RULES OF TEAM COALITION’S RESPONSIBILITY HAS ITS REWARDS PROGRAM WITH TEXAS CHRISTIAN UNIVERSITY. NO PURCHASE IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.

Eligibility: TEAM Coalition’s Responsible Fan Program for the 2026 Season ("Promotion") is open only to natural persons who are legal residents of the fifty (50) United States of America or the District of Columbia who are twenty-one (21) years of age or older at the time of entry. Promotion may only be entered from and in eligible jurisdictions. Employees, directors, and officers of Promotion Entities (defined below) and their respective parent, subsidiary and affiliate companies, employees, agents, and suppliers, as well as the immediate family (spouse, parents, siblings, and children), household members and friends of each such employee are not eligible to participate. In the event of a dispute as to the identity of an entrant, the authorized account holder of the account will be deemed to be the entrant. Administrator (defined below) may request any potential winner to provide proof that such person is the winner. Potential winner is subject to verification before any prize will be awarded. Subject to all applicable, federal, state, and local laws and regulations. Void where prohibited. Participation constitutes entrant's full and unconditional agreement to these Official Rules and Administrator's decisions, which are final and binding in all matters related to the Promotion. Winning a prize is contingent upon fulfilling all requirements set forth herein.

Sponsor: The sponsors of the Promotion are Molson Coors and Texas Christian University ("Sponsors").

Administrator: The Administrator of the Promotion is TEAM Coalition, Inc. ("Administrator"). Sponsors and Administrator are collectively referred to herein as "Promotion Entities".

Timing: The Promotion Period for the sweepstakes starts at 12:01 AM CT on Saturday September 12, 2026. The Promotion Period sweepstakes ends at 11:59 PM CT on Saturday November 21, 2026.

Administrator's clock is the official time-keeping device of the Promotion.

How To Enter: Complete the Promotion entry form at https://teamcoalition.org/sweepstakes/ncaa/tcu-registration/.For all entries: Each participant is eligible to submit up to one (1) Promotion entry for each of the six (6) TCU home football games during the 2026 season. Multiple entrants are not permitted to share the same email address or phone account. Any attempt by any entrant to obtain more than the stated number of entries by using multiple/different email addresses/phone numbers, identities, registrations and logins, or any other methods will void that entrant's entries and that entrant will be disqualified. Use of any automated technology or system to participate is prohibited and will result in disqualification. Any automated receipt (such as one confirming delivery of text message or email) does not constitute proof of actual receipt by Administrator of an entry. Promotion Entities are not responsible for lost, late, incomplete, invalid, unintelligible or misdirected entries/registrations, all of which will be disqualified. All entries become the property of the Administrator and none will be acknowledged or returned.

Prizes are provided by Sponsors. Fulfillment for Grand Prizes will be completed by the Administrator. Administrator reserves the right to revoke full or partial prize from any winner who it or Sponsor’s representatives deem may, in their sole discretion, be intoxicated, be a safety risk, have violated any venue policy or law, or may bring Administrator or Sponsor into disrepute. An unclaimed and/or unused prize element will remain the property of the Sponsor.  The cash value, in lieu of the actual prize, will not be awarded.

Odds of winning a prize depends on the number of eligible entries received. Lost or stolen prize will not be replaced. Prize does not include any other item or expense not specifically described in these Official Rules, and any such additional expenses are the sole responsibility of winner. Prize may not be transferred or assigned. Only listed prize will be awarded and no other prize or substitutions will be made except at the sole discretion of Administrator. Winner is solely responsible for reporting and paying any and all applicable taxes. Winner may be required to provide Administrator with a valid taxpayer identification number or social security number before any prize will be awarded. A Form 1099 will be issued to the winner for value of prize $600 or more.

Selection and Verification of Potential Winners: On or about Monday November 30, 2026, two (2) Grand Prize winners will be randomly selected from all eligible entries. Administrator reserves the right to disqualify any participant or winner and may refuse to award any prize to a person who is ineligible or has violated any rule, gained unfair advantage in participating in the Promotion, or obtained winner status using fraudulent means.

Potential winner will be notified by email.
