Since September 2011, NASCAR has been an incredibly active member of TEAM Coalition. The two organizations have worked together to expand the reach of the designated driver program across the country and encourage thousands of race fans to pledge to be designated drivers.

In past seasons, NASCAR and TEAM have rewarded fans who have taken the Responsibility Has Its Rewards pledge. Each fan who had taken the RHIR pledge at a race where TEAM was present or pledged online was entered for a chance to win three different prizes.

  1. The Grand Prize was a trip for two fans to the  NASCAR Cup Series Championship Race.
  2. The First Prize was a trip for two to the NASCAR All-Star Race.
  3. The Responsible Fan / Designated Driver for the Race prize consisted of VIP Access for two fans and tickets for two to the next NASCAR Cup Series race to be held at the track where the winner was selected as the Responsible Fan/ Designated Driver for the Race.
Fans who pledge to be responsible drinkers and designated drivers pledge to prevent drunk driving, always have a designated driver and ensure that everyone always buckles up.
