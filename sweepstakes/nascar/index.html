Responsibility Has Its Rewards Sweepstakes

The Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes with NASCAR is closed until the current season resumes play. Thank you for your commitment to preventing drunk driving and for being a responsible NASCAR fan. TEAM Coalition and NASCAR support the responsible fan and designated driver programs at NASCAR Cup Series™ races at many races across the country each season. Fans who pledge to prevent drunk driving by serving as designated driver or having a plan to get home safely are entered for a change to win. The Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes runs independently at specific NASCAR Cup Series races. Each fan who takes the RHIR pledge at a race where TEAM is present or pledges online is entered for a chance to win a pair of tickets and VIP Credentials for a future NASCAR Cup Series race. All Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes winners must meet sweepstakes requirements in order to be eligible for prizes. Click here to view past RHIR Sweepstakes with NASCAR. TEAM Coalition and our partners will be running responsible fan/designated driver booths at many races in 2022. Before the green flag waves, stop by to take the pledge to never drive drunk, and you'll find that Responsibility has its Rewards!
