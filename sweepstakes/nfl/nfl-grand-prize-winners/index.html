TEAM Coalition and the National Football League are supporting the responsible fan and designated driver programs at all NFL stadiums every season with a special incentive. Every stadium offers fans a responsible fan or designated driver program. Fans who pledge to be a designated driver or have a plan to avoid drunk driving are registered for the sweepstakes.

At the end of the season, one responsible fan/designated driver from each team will be selected as the Responsible Fan for the Season. The Responsible Fans for the Season from the teams that compete in Super Bowl will each receive the Grand Prize of the Sweepstakes. The Grand Prize of the Sweepstakes is a trip for two (2) to the Super Bowl, including two (2) tickets, round trip airfare, and hotel accommodations (if necessary).

A First Prize for the RHIR sweepstakes is also awarded. The Responsible Fans for the Season whose teams do not compete in the postseason are qualified to win the first prize. The first prize is a trip to Pro Bowl. That winner will be chosen randomly from the Responsible Fans for the Season not competing in Super Bowl. The Pro Bowl trip includes two (2) tickets to the game, round trip airfare, and hotel accommodations (if necessary).
