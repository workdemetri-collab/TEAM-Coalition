Responsibility Has Its Rewards Sweepstakes

TEAM Coalition and the National Football League are supporting the responsible fan programs at all NFL stadiums every season with a special incentive. Every stadium offers fans a responsible fan program. Fans who register to be a designated driver pledge to not drink alcohol, drive their friends and family home safely, and ensure that everyone always buckles up. Fan who are over 21 years of age who plan to drink alcohol at the game, pledge to have a designated driver to ensure that they get home safely. At the end of the season, one responsible fan from each team will be selected as the Responsible Fan for the Season. The Responsible Fans for the Season from the teams will be finalists in the sweepstakes. Two Grand Prizes of the Sweepstakes for the 2026 season are: A trip for two (2) to the 2027 Draft Day, including roundtrip airfare for two (2) and double-occupancy hotel accommodations for three (3) nights in Washington, DC. A trip for two (2) to the 2027 Hall of Fame Game, including a pair of tickets to the 2027 Hall of Fame Game, airfare for two (2) and double-occupancy hotel accommodations for three (3) nights in Canton, OH. The winners will be chosen randomly from the qualifying Responsible Fans for the Season. Entrants for the Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes are all NFL fans who pledged to be responsible and prevent drunk driving at an NFL stadium during the regular season. Entry is also available online at www.rhir.org or by mail.
