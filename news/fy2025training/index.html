    8. The Kraft Group, Gillette Stadium - 496
    9. Gourmet Dining, Rutgers - 422
    10. Arizona Diamondbacks, Chase Field - 352

The 39,967 employees certified in 2025 represents an 8% decrease from the previous fiscal year.

Online Level 2 (for alcohol servers) represents 76% of all employee TEAM certification in 2025.

Employee Certification represents 72% of TEAM's total annual revenue. Both manuals sold and online accesses purchased increased in 2025 relative to 2024. The 19,850 manuals sold is a 30% increase, and the 33,115 online accesses purchased is a 7% increase.
