Alcohol Responsibility at the 2017 World Baseball Classic

TEAM Coalition traveled to Marlins Park in Miami, FL, Petco Park in San Diego, CA and Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, CA for the 2017 World Baseball Classic. More than 1,800 baseball fans pledged to always have a designated driver.
