Under the guidance of our Marketing & Communications Committee Chair, Sheley Riester (Anheuser-Busch), TEAM accomplished a great deal in the last year. More than 280,000 sports and entertainment fans pledged to be responsible drinkers and prevent drunk driving. TEAM Coalition managed the digital pledge program for 38 venues, teams, and events including the Houston Rodeo, Super Bowl LX, 13 NFL teams, 6 Live Nation amphitheaters, 4 NBA teams, 4 colleges, 2 MLS clubs, and 2 NHL teams.

LIVE NATION AND NHTSA

The fiscal year ending on 9/30/2026 marked the fifth and final year of an investment among TEAM Coalition, NHTSA (National Highway Traffic Safety Administration), and Live Nation. Throughout the summer months, LiveNation.com and TicketMaster.com websites displayed advertisements aligned with the traffic safety campaign calendar. The four campaigns earned more than 3.6 million impressions and nearly 1,200 clicks from June through September from a $40,000 investment.
Campaign Dates Impressions Clicks Click Thru Rate
Take Care of Your Crew 6/5 - 6/16 909,438 244 0.03%
Buzzed Driving is Drunk Driving 6/26 - 7/6 919,133 324 0.04%
Buckle Up 7/17 - 8/4 918,132 270 0.03%
Drive Sober or Get Pulled Over 9/2 - 9/9 915,217 336 0.04%
Total 3,661,920 1,174 0.03%
With Blossom Music Center (a Live Nation venue where Legends Global is the concessionaire), TEAM tested the impact of social media advertisements to support the designated driver program, sponsored by the Ohio Department of Public Safety. Fan participation in the program increased 19% when the Instagram ads ran.
