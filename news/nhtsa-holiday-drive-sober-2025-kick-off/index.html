NHTSA Holiday Drive Sober 2025 Kick Off

TEAM Coalition proudly joined the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Aramark, MADD, the Fairfax County Police Department, and Brian Baldinger (https://x.com/BaldyNFL) to kick off NHTSA’s annual Winter Holidays Impaired Driving high-visibility enforcement campaign at District E in Washington, D.C. on Tuesday, December 9 at 11 a.m. ET.

Speakers included:

  • Jonathan Morrison, NHTSA Administrator;
  • Robert A. Blakley, Assistant Chief, Fairfax County Police Department;
  • Alison Birdwell, Aramark Sports + Entertainment President;
  • Brian Baldinger, former NFL player and current commentator and analyst; and
  • Michele Mirowicz Hobart, MADD advocate.
Click on the image below to view the remarks from the press event.

NHTSA’s Drive Sober or Get Pulled Over and Drive High, Get a DUI campaigns begin on December 10, 2025, and run through January 1, 2026. Click here to download campaign materials.
