NFL, Bud Light and TEAM at 2017 NFL Experience

TEAM Coalition traveled to Houston, TX for the 2017 NFL Experience leading up to Super Bowl 51 featuring the New England Patriots vs the Atlanta Falcons. For nine days, from Saturday, January 28 through Sunday February 5, 2017, TEAM and Bud Light rewarded fans who made the Responsibility Has Its Rewards pledge.
