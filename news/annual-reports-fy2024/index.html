FISCAL YEAR 2024 - Year in Review

43,000 employees - staffing 135 sports and entertainment venues across North America - became TEAM certified. More than 160,000 sports and entertainment fans pledged to be responsible drinkers and prevent drunk driving in the last year. TEAM Coalition managed the digital pledge program for 23 venues and teams. TEAM Coalition welcomed one new member organization in 2024 - New Belgium Brewing Company. Finally, TEAM Coalition ended the year with $150,000 in net income, more than $750,000 cash on hand, and total assets of nearly $900,000.
