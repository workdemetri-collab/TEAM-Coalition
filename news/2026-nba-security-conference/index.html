Sep 3, 2026 · NBA, TEAM Coalition News

TEAM at NBA Security Conference

TEAM attended the 2026 NBA Security Conference in Atlanta, GA from 8/31/2026 to 9/2/2026. It was good to be back.

We connected with NBA league, team, and venue professionals on the importance of alcohol management training, responsible alcohol consumption, and rewarding responsible behavior. TEAM had the opportunity to recognize the following teams and venues on their exceptional efforts in responsibility.

Congratulations again to the 2025 TEAM Coalition Award Winners:

Top 5 Across all TEAM Customers for Employees Certified New York Knicks - Madison Square Garden

2025 NBA Top 5 for Employees Certified Los Angeles Clippers - Intuit Dome Cleveland Cavaliers, Aramark - Rocket Arena

2025 Multi-Sport Top 5 for Employees Certified Denver Nuggets, Kroenke Sports & Entertainment, Legends Global - Ball Arena Washington Wizards, Monumental Sports & Entertainment, Aramark - Capital One Arena

#1 NBA Team for Responsible Fan Pledges Dallas Mavericks - American Airlines Center

Thank you to the NBA for hosting us, all the security and operations friends who stopped by to chat, and to our award winners! The commitment to our shared goal was clear: reducing negative alcohol-related incidents in and around sports and entertainment facilities.

For more information on TEAM training and awards please visit: https://teamcoalition.org/news/fy2025training/

TEAM Coalition is an alliance of professional and collegiate sports, entertainment facilities, concessionaires, stadium service providers, the beer industry, distillers, broadcasters, traffic safety experts and others working together to promote responsible drinking and positive fan behavior at sports and entertainment facilities. TEAM Coalition members and supporters include National Basketball Association, Anheuser-Busch Companies, Aramark, Major League Soccer, NASCAR, National Football League, National Hockey League, National Collegiate Athletic Association, Legends Global, Oak View Group, Beer Institute, National Beer Wholesalers Association, Constellation Brands, Molson Coors, Brown-Forman, Live Nation, International Association of Venue Managers, Stadium Managers Association and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. For more information visit www.TEAMCoalition.org or www.FansDontLetFansDriveDrunk.org.
