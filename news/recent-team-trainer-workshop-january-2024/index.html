TEAM Train-the-Trainer Workshop January 9-11, 2024

Thank you to all the participants from our recent Instructor Development Program (IDP) workshop on January 9-11, 2024!

The thirty workshop participants represented Aramark, Legends, Oak View Group, Chicago Cubs, St. Louis CITY SC, Hampton Coliseum, Argus, Monterrey Security and S.A.F.E. Management.

Click here to see the schedule of upcoming TEAM Instructor Development Programs.

To find out more about the TEAM alcohol management training program, please visit: teamcoalition.org/training.
