(Left to Right) Jeff Jannarone, Best Bar None; Sheley Riester, Anheuser-Busch; Carolyn Colton, Aramark; Bill Young, Beer Institute; Kelly Roberson, Center for Alcohol Policy; Kevin Tedesco, Aramark; Chloe Janfaza, Las Vegas Raiders; Kevin Bruder, IAVM; Jill Kiefer, TEAM Coalition; Jeana Schultz, SMA; Alison Hanrahan, Molson Coors; Lester Jones, NBWA; Bill Elliott, The HERO Campaign for Designated Drivers; Phil Del Giudice, Oak View Group; Christy Verbosky, TEAM Coalition; Lina Baquero, Delaware North; and Jess Thurston, TEAM Coalition

Aramark at Citizens Bank Park hosted TEAM Coalition stakeholders in the Aramark suite on Tuesday 4/29/2025 for the Phillies game vs. the Washington Nationals. Aramark made the event unforgettable. The Phillies bottom of the 9th victory added an extra dose of magic. The responsible fan program at Citizens Bank Park is a partnership featuring the Decide to Ride campaign supported by Anheuser-Busch, MADD, and Uber as well as Responsibility Has Its Rewards, a TEAM Coalition program and The HERO Campaign for Designated Drivers.At every Phillies home game at Citizens Bank Park, a lucky and responsible fan wins a $150 Phillies Gift Card as the winner of the Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes.

Thank you to all the Board members, member organization representatives, Strategic Partners, and guests who joined us for the TEAM Coalition Stakeholders meeting on Wednesday, April 30, 2025 at Citizens Bank Park.
