Delaware North Sportservice and TEAM Coalition

Delaware North Sportservice is a long-time member of TEAM Coalition. The two organizations have worked together to train tens of thousands of venue employees in Techniques for Effective Alcohol Management. Delaware North Sportservice also aids in the management of designated driver programs in facilities where it operates as the concessionaire.

In 2017, a total of 11,131 Delaware North Sportservice employees became TEAM certified and 31 Delaware North Sportservice employees became TEAM Trainers.

Delaware North Sportservice is a regular recipient of TEAM Training Awards recognizing its commitment to responsible alcohol management. These are listed below. You can find more details on our Training Awards page.
