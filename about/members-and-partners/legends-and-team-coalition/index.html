Legends and TEAM Coalition

Legends officially joined TEAM Coalition in 2015. But Legends had been an active TEAM training customer for several years before becoming a member of the organization.  In 2017, a total of 9,400 Legends employees became TEAM certified and 32 Legend employees became TEAM Trainers. The majority of those certified were from Live Nation venues.

Several Legends facilities have received TEAM Training Awards recognizing their commitment to responsible alcohol management. These are listed below. You can find more details on our Training Awards page.
