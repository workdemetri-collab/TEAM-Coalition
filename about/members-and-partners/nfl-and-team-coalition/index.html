The National Football League is a founding member of TEAM Coalition. Since 2001, the two organizations have worked together to train tens of thousands of stadium employees in alcohol management and to encourage fans to pledge to be designated drivers.

Every stadium offers fans a designated driver program. Fans who register to be a designated driver pledge to not drink alcohol, drive their friends and family home safely, and ensure that everyone always buckles up. In return for their pledge, these fans are entered to win the Responsibility Has Its Rewards (RHIR) sweepstakes.

Track league-wide designated driver pledges and check the standings of Budweiser Good Sport Designated Driver Challenges on the NFL Fans Don’t Let Fans Drive Drunk page.

Every year, TEAM Coalition recognizes the teams, concessionaires, and other game day staffing companies who have shown an outstanding effort in making sure their employees are certified in effective alcohol management. Award winners are listed in the table below:

2020

2020 NFL Operations Training Winners
Three categories of awards were given this year. The Prestigious Achievement award recognizes those organizations in which at least 90% of TOTAL STAFF is TEAM certified. The Championship Performance category recognizes those organizations which, in 2018 alone, certified 100% of the operations staff necessary to operate the building for an average event. The Excellent Performer category recognizes those organizations which, in 2018, certified more than 50% of the operations staff necessary to operate the building for an average event.
