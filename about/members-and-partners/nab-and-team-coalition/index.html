NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF BROADCASTERS AND TEAM

NAB is a long-time member of TEAM Coalition. The two organizations have worked together to bring TEAM's messages of responsibility to a wider audience through NAB's network of TV and radio broadcasters.

In 2019, TEAM attended the NAB Show in Las Vegas, NV and sent materials to the NAB Radio Show in Dallas, TX.

TEAM has television and radio PSAs available for download on the NAB Public Service Spot Center.

MARKETING CAMPAIGNS

RESPONSIBILITY HAS ITS REWARDS

The Responsibility Has Its Rewards (RHIR) sweepstakes is TEAM Coalition's longest-running program to recognize fans for having a plan to avoid drunk driving. Fans who pledge to be a designated driver or have a plan to get home safely are entered for a chance to win tickets or a trip to the leagues' championship games or premier events.

TEAM Coalition is willing and eager to spread the RHIR message. TEAM member organizations are incorporating the Responsibility Has Its Rewards program in more and more events, like the Indianapolis 500 with MillerCoors and boxing events with Constellation Brands.

If you are interested in partnering with TEAM Coalition to introduce the Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes - or expand upon an existing program to reward your guests for making responsible decisions with alcohol - please contact us today!
