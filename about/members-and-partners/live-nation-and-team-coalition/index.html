Live Nation has been a long-time member of TEAM Coalition. Since 2009, Live Nation, TEAM, the HERO Campaign and the U.S. Department of Transportation National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) have worked together to offer concert goers the designated driver program and the Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes.

Thousands of music lovers have also made the pledge to stay sober and ensure a safe ride home from the concert for their friends and family. Over the last three years, Live Nation also prioritized certifying employees in TEAM's effective alcohol management training program. A total of 168 Live Nation employees became TEAM certified in 2017. In addition, 6,686 Legends and 429 Aramark employees of Live Nation venues became TEAM certified in 2017. For both organizations, that is a huge commitment to TEAM Coalition. Those efforts play a crucial role in ensuring guests enjoy alcohol responsibly and that everyone gets home safely.

2021 Live Nation Concessionaire Training Winners
During the 2020 fiscal year, concessionaire companies continued to post strong numbers of TEAM-certified employees. TEAM awards include two categories.

The Championship Performance category is defined by training 500 employees as the primary concessionaire or 250 employees as a premium concessionaire at a Live Nation venue.

The Excellent Performer category is defined by training 250 employees as the primary concessionaire or 125 employees as a premium concessionaire at a Live Nation venue.
