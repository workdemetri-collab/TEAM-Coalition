Aramark and TEAM Coalition

Aramark is a founding member of TEAM Coalition. The two organizations have worked together to train tens of thousands of employees in TEAM's effective alcohol management training program. Aramark also manages the designated driver programs in the facilities where it operates as the concessionaire. In addition, Aramark honors thousands of complimentary soft drink coupons each year as part of the designated driver program.

The President of Aramark Sports and Entertainment, Carl Mittleman, serves as the Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors for TEAM Coalition. The TEAM Coalition Board includes four officers, three committee chairs and a director at large. The eight-member board meets twice a year in person in addition to monthly conference calls to help guide the organization.

In 2017, a total of 14,096 Aramark employees became TEAM certified and 75 Aramark employees became TEAM Trainers.
