TEAM Coalition and NASCAR partnered on 29 events days promoting alcohol responsibility in the 2019 fiscal year. A total of 6.761 photos were taken. Each included a message about drunk driving prevention or wearing your seat belt. Those photos were viewed a total of 23,525 times. That is an average of 3.5 views per photo.
