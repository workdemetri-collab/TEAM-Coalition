TEAM Coalition and Anheuser-Busch partnered on 61 events promoting alcohol responsibility in the current fiscal year. A total of 7,941 photos have been taken. Each included a message about drunk driving prevention or wearing your seat belt. Those photos were viewed a total of 26,552 times. That is an average of more than three views per photo.
