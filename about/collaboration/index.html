Collaboration

TEAM is committed to ensuring all sports and entertainment facilities have the most comprehensive list of Reasonable Efforts in place to provide the safest possible event day experience for both employees and guests.

Alcohol Management Certification for facility employees should be customized to teach the policies and procedures the employees will be expected to enforce at the type of events for which they will be working. The training should be interactive, informative and easily accessible.

Consumer programs should be turn-key. They should target the demographic most likely to mis-use alcohol - men between the ages of 21 and 35 (according to the U.S. Department of Transportation National Highway Traffic Safety Administration).  Whenever possible, consumer programs should highlight the partnership between teams, facility operators, concessionaires and alcohol sponsors.

TEAM is committed to ensuring that best practices in alcohol management are shared across the sports and entertainment industry. TEAM provides the following services:

Responsibility Program Toolkit

TEAM provides a responsiblity program implementation toolkit for stadiums, arenas, concert venues and  race tracks. The toolkit provides the following:
  • Data collection
  • Partner roles
  • RHIR Sweepstakes

Venue Alcohol Service Policies

Before the start of each season, TEAM collects and updates the alcohol service policies for all venues in all professional sports leagues that are members of TEAM Coalition. Those reports are only available to member organizations.
