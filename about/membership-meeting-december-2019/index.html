FAN EDUCATION

TEAM Coalition and our member organizations have partnered throughout the last fiscal year 2019 (October 1, 2018 to September 30, 2019) promoting alcohol responsibility. From the 99 days of activations where TEAM Coalition staffers were on-site, a total of 16,410 photos were taken and 33,348 fans made the pledge to prevent drunk driving. Across all TEAM Coalition member leagues, teams and venues, a total of 1,036,651 fans pledged to be responsible drinkers or designated drivers during the last 12 months. TEAM Coalition also manages Responsibility Has Its Rewards sweepstakes with MLB, MLS, NASCAR, NBA, NFL, NHL and Constellation Brands. TEAM and NCAA partnered on the TEAM Up 2 Win! sweepstakes for the Men's and Women's Final Four. And TEAM offered the Raise One Million ARMs sweepstakes with all member organizations.
