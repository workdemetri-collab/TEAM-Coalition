Thank you to all the Board members, member organization representatives, Strategic Partners, and Training Committee Members who joined us for the TEAM Coalition Stakeholders meeting on Thursday, July 23, 2026.

The ballots are closed - all votes have been approved.

APPROVED: FY 2027 Budget

Yes 12 No 0 Abstain 0
The FY 2027 approved budget accounts for the slower than expected sales of both participant manuals and accesses to online training that we are experiencing in FY 2026. Expected revenue is $140,000 less than the FY 2026 budget. The only new expense for FY 2027 is a website make0ver. It has been more than a decade since the website has had any significant style changes.

APPROVED: Treasurer - Lester Jones, NBWA

Yes 12 No 0 Abstain 0
Lester will serve another 3-year term on the Board of Directors. Under Lester's leadership, TEAM has been debt-free since 2023, maintained net assets of nearly $1 million, and kept expenses under budget.The Vice-Chair board position is still vacant. Please email Jill Kiefer (jill@teamcoalition.org) if you would like to serve or recommend someone to serve.

APPROVED: New Strategic Partner - NLLEA

Yes 10 No 1 Abstain 1
The National Liquor Law Enforcement Association (NLLEA) is a nonprofit professional association dedicated to advancing the enforcement and regulation of alcohol beverage laws. Our membership includes law enforcement, regulatory, and compliance professionals from federal, state, and local agencies who share a commitment to promoting public safety through effective alcohol regulation and enforcement. TEAM is in the early stages of learning more about NLLEA. TEAM plans to attend the NLLEA 2026 Conference at the end of September in New Orleans to learn more about the organization.

Approved: New Strategic Partner - Safe Bar Network

Yes 11 No 0 Abstain 1
The SAFE Bar Network is a nonprofit focused on partnering with alcohol-serving venues to help them increase profit, increase repeat customers, and decrease employee turnover. We achieve these goals by training their employees to use active bystander skills and de-escalation to proactively prevent overconsumption, harassment, and other forms of harm. TEAM plans to make a formal announcement in early August.
