Facility operations, concessions, security and human resources managers attend a two-day train-the-trainer workshop called an Instructor Development Program (IDP) in order to become a certified TEAM trainer. Currently, TEAM has over 1,200 active trainers, all full-time managers of sports and entertainment facilities.
