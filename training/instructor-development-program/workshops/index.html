Instructor Development Program (IDP) Workshops

Purchase TEAM Training ProductsFacility operations, concessions, security and human resources managers attend a train-the-trainer workshop called an Instructor Development Program (IDP) in order to become a certified TEAM trainer. Currently, TEAM has over 1,000 active trainers, all full-time managers of sports and entertainment facilities. After completing the workshop and passing the certification test, trainers train the TEAM program to their employees. Recertification every two years.

Hosting an IDP

We are always looking for facilities to host these two-day, train-the-trainer workshops. The host receives free registration(s). Please let us know if you are interested in hosting a future IDP workshop.The standard registration fee for an IDP is $750. Discounts are available based on membership in TEAM Coalition. Click here for all training prices.
