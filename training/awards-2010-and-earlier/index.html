TEAM Training Awards

TEAM Coalition recognizes sports teams, concessionaires, and individual trainers for their commitment to responsible alcohol management. Sports team and concessions winners are selected based on the number of staff trained in effective alcohol management relative to the total number of event employees. Trainers are recognized for the total number of employees they certify in the TEAM program.

2010 Operations Winners

In fiscal year 2010, more operations staff members of sports teams than ever were certified in TEAM Coalition's alcohol management program across the leagues and nation. Three categories of awards were given this year. The Prestigious Achievement award recognizes those organizations in which at least 90% of TOTAL STAFF is TEAM certified. The Championship Performance category recognizes those organizations which, in 2010 alone, certified 100% of the operations staff necessary to operate the building for an average event. The Excellent Performer category recognizes those organizations which, in 2010, certified more than 50% of the operations staff necessary to operate the building for an average event.
