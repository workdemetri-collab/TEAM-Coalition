Alcohol By Volume

Alcohol By Volume (ABV) is the percent of pure alcohol in the drink. The higher the ABV, the more quickly the drinker’s Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) will rise. And a high BAC is when things can get dangerous, like driving drunk.
